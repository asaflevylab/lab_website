## Students from all levels (M.Sc., Ph.D., and postdocs)

Students with background and/or interest in at least one of these fields are
invited to apply with their CV and transcripts:

- Molecular Biology
- Computational Biology
- Microbiology
- Plant Biology

Students with strong quantitative/computational backgrounds are also invited to
apply if they are interested in and excited by the lab's area of study.

Write to [{{< email >}}](mailto:{{< email >}}) with your CV and transcripts.
